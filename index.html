How the collection works

Three rules. All three are deliberate, and two of them are the interesting ones.

It only ever grows

The tray counts gift cards received, not money held. Spend your whole balance and your pastries stay. A good-service card from last spring is still on the shelf.

$50 received = 1 pastry

The threshold is hidden

It names what’s coming — never how far away it is. Staff can’t speed up getting a card, so a progress bar would only be noise. Hidden, it stays a gift instead of an accounting statement.

“Egg Tart is in the oven”

Nobody’s shelf matches

The order is seeded from the staff number, so the same balance looks different on every phone. It turned into something people compare on shift — which was the point.
